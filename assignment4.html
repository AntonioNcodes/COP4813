Assignment 4 - Creating Plots

This assignment uses JavaScript to calculate compound interest and display the results on a graph.

The equation used is: A = P(1 + r/n)nt

A is the final amount, P is the initial amount, r is the annual interest rate, n is the number of times interest is compounded per year, and t is the time in years.
