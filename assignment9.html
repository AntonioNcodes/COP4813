How to Play

  1. Watch the golf ball move diagonally and bounce when the game loads.
  2. Click the ball to see a visual flash.
  3. Drag from the ball in the direction you want to shoot, then release.
  4. Wait for the ball to stop before taking your next shot.
  5. Sand bunkers slow the ball down. Try to reach the hole in as few shots as possible.
  6. Choose New Game to restart.

About This Assignment

This project demonstrates JavaScript animation and client-side interactivity using the HTML5 canvas. The golf ball moves diagonally and bounces off the boundaries during the opening animation. Players can click the ball to trigger a visible effect and drag to aim their shots. The game also demonstrates motion, boundary detection, friction, and mouse events.
