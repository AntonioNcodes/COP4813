How to Play

  1. Watch the opening loading screen and bouncing-ball demo.
  2. Click the moving demo ball to change its color and direction.
  3. After the mini-golf course appears, press and drag backward from the golf ball.
  4. Release to shoot toward the hole.
  5. Avoid rocks and water, and collect power-ups and coins.
  6. Get the ball into the hole to complete the level.
  7. Click Next Level to continue after completing a level.

About This Assignment

This project demonstrates JavaScript animation using the HTML5 canvas. It includes a bouncing object, mouse interaction, ball movement, collision detection, obstacles, power-ups, scoring, and three mini-golf levels.
