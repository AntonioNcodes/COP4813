How to Play

  1. Wait for the opening animation to finish.
  2. Click and drag from the golf ball to aim your shot.
  3. Release the mouse button to shoot. Drag farther to increase your power.
  4. Avoid rocks and water hazards. Sand traps slow your ball down.
  5. Collect power-ups to improve your shots.
  6. Get the ball into the hole to complete each level.
  7. Complete all three levels to win the game!

Your Three Challenges

Level 1 - Beginner

Practice aiming and controlling your shot. Navigate the sand trap and collect a golden power-up to help reach the hole.

Level 2 - The Challenge

Avoid rock obstacles and additional sand traps. Plan your shots carefully and look for a speed power-up.

Level 3 - The Final Hole

Take on the most difficult course, featuring water hazards, rocks, and sand traps. Finish all three levels to see your final score.

About This Assignment

This project demonstrates client-side JavaScript and HTML5 canvas animation. During the opening animation, the golf ball moves diagonally and bounces off the boundaries. The game then allows the player to aim and shoot using mouse or pointer events.

The game also demonstrates collision detection, movement, friction, power-ups, score tracking, and progression through three levels.
