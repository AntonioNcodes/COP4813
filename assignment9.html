LIVE AQUARIUM

The aquarium is alive! Click an animal or release some food.

How to Interact

  • Click a fish: It darts away in a burst of light.
  • Click a jellyfish: It pulses with a glowing ripple.
  • Click a turtle: It changes direction and glows.
  • Click a manta ray: It performs a quick glide.
  • Click a seahorse: It spirals upward briefly.
  • Click a shark: It makes a quick evasive turn.
  • Click empty water: Release glowing food that fish can chase.

About This Animation

This project uses the HTML canvas and JavaScript to draw and animate six kinds of marine animals. Timer-based animation updates their positions, keeps them inside the aquarium, and creates glowing effects. Mouse-click events allow the viewer to interact with the animals and their environment.
