Mini Golf Challenge

Welcome to the practice green! Aim your shot, control your power, and try to get the golf ball into the hole in as few attempts as possible.

How to Play

  1. Place your pointer on the golf ball.
  2. Click or touch and drag toward where you want to shoot.
  3. Release to hit the ball. A longer drag creates a stronger shot.
  4. Wait for the ball to stop before taking your next shot.
  5. Get the ball into the hole to win!

About This Assignment

This project demonstrates client-side JavaScript and HTML5 canvas animation. Players aim and shoot a golf ball using mouse or touch events. The ball moves with simulated friction, bounces off the canvas boundaries, and responds to user interaction. The objective is to reach the golf hole in as few shots as possible.
