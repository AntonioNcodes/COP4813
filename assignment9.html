How to Play

  1. Wait for the opening animation to finish.
  2. Click or touch the golf ball.
  3. Drag backward in the opposite direction you want to shoot.
  4. Release to shoot the ball.
  5. Avoid rocks and water, and collect power-ups.
  6. Get the ball into the hole to complete each level.
  7. Click Next Level when you finish a level.

About This Assignment

This project demonstrates JavaScript animation using the HTML5 canvas. It includes an animated golf ball, mouse and touch controls, collision detection, obstacles, sand friction, water hazards, power-ups, scoring, and three playable levels.
